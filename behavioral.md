# 🎯 1. Client & Stakeholder Management

## Core Questions

* Tell me about a time you handled a **difficult client or stakeholder**
* How do you **manage conflicting requirements** from stakeholders?
* Describe a situation where **client expectations were unclear**
* How do you ensure **alignment between technical and business teams?**
* Have you ever had to **push back on a client requirement? How?**

---

## Deep-Dive Questions

* How do you handle **last-minute requirement changes**?
* Tell me about a time when a **stakeholder was unhappy with your solution**
* How do you prioritize when **multiple stakeholders demand urgency?**

# 🎯 2. Communication & Presentation Skills

## Core Questions

* Explain a complex system (like your AutoResolveAI or RAG system) to a **non-technical audience**
